discussbooks

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Who uses discussbooks

discussbooks is a private tool used only by its owner, who signs in with their own Google account. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What discussbooks accesses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, discussbooks uses YouTube API Services to work with the owner's own channels. It uploads videos, edits their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, reads basic channel and video details, and posts the channel's own comment on the owner's videos.

Before posting that comment, discussbooks may read the public comments on the owner's own video to check that its comment is not already there. It does not store or share those comments.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in tokens Google issues are kept on the owner's computer and in the owner's private cloud storage. discussbooks does not sell, share or transfer any data, and it collects no information about anyone other than its owner.

The use of information received from Google APIs by discussbooks adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

discussbooks uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the third-party connections page of your Google account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the discussbooks Google sign-in screen.
